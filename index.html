BigShopR — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This describes exactly what BigShopR collects, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written from what the app actually does, not from intent.

BigShopR is operated by Sreekumar Sasidharan, an individual developer based in India. For any question about this policy or your data: contact@bigshopr.com.

What we collect

Account

When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive, from that provider:

DataWhy
A user IDIdentifies your account and controls what you can read
Email addressDistinguishes accounts; never shown to other users
Display nameShown to people you share a list with
Profile photo URLShown to people you share a list with

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address Apple gives us. We never receive your password — authentication is handled by Google, Apple and Firebase Authentication.

Your lists

Item names, quantities, units, categories, priority, who added each item, whether it has been bought and when. Plus list names and the members of each list.

This is visible to every member of a list you share. That is the purpose of the app, but it is worth being explicit: anyone you invite can see everything on that list, including items added before they joined.

Your location

Only if you allow it, and only while you are using the app. The app asks the operating system for a "balanced" fix, accurate to roughly a hundred metres, and it is stored as latitude and longitude.

It is used for one thing: working out how far away a shop is. When you send your list to a shop, that position is attached to the request. The shop's screen shows it as a distance — "1.6 km away" — but the coordinates are stored on that request, and the shop's account can read it, so treat it as the shop knowing roughly where you were when you sent the list. If you refuse the permission the app carries on, sends no position and shows no distance.

Your addresses and phone number

You can save addresses and a contact number in the app. They are stored in a private area of your account that nobody else can read — not other members of your lists, and not shops.

You can also choose one of your saved addresses for a list. A copy is then kept on that list, so the app can use it for delivery, and the other members of that list can see it.

A delivery address is shared with a shop only if you choose delivery when you accept the shop's reply. It is saved on that order, and the shop can read it only once you have accepted. For a pickup order the shop never receives an address or your phone number.

If you register a shop, the shop's own name, phone number and address are different: those are business details, shown to any household that finds your shop, which is the point of listing it.

Your orders

When you send a list to a shop, the order records the items and quantities, your display name and your household's name, which shop it went to, the shop's reply (what is in stock, any substitute it offered, and the price it put on each item), the order's status and times, and the four-digit code used at handover. BigShopR does not take payment or card details — a price here is only what the shop says the item costs, and you pay the shop yourself.

Orders you have sent stay on your account and on the shop's side as a record of the transaction, as set out under How long we keep it below.

Notifications

Only if you allow them. The app stores a token that identifies your phone to the notification service (Apple's and Google's push services, through Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your time zone, so notifications can be delivered and not sent in the middle of the night. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Photos and feedback

If you run a shop you can add a storefront photo, which is stored with your shop and shown to households that find it. The app asks for access to your photo library only when you choose to add one. If you use Report a problem, the message you write is stored with your display name, the list you were in, and your app version and platform, so we can look into it.

Your camera

Only if you allow it, and only while you are scanning a shop's code. The camera feed is read on your phone to find the code and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. If you refuse the permission you can type the code instead.

Diagnostics

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and usage events (Firebase Analytics). Both are tied to your account's user ID, so a crash or a pattern of use can be traced to an account when we investigate it. Events record that something happened — an item was added, a list created — never what the item was. Analytics is disabled in development builds.

What does not leave your device

QR codes are generated on your phone. BigShopR does not record audio, does not use your microphone, and sends nothing to any service other than the processors named above.

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act gives you the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your personal data
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you are incapacitated
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response

To exercise any of these, email contact@bigshopr.com. We aim to respond within 30 days. Sreekumar Sasidharan is the point of contact for all data questions.

Deleting your account removes your profile and your membership of every list. Items you added to a shared list remain on that list, because other members rely on them and they are no longer solely yours — they will show as added by a removed member.

International transfers

Firebase stores data on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be outside India. Google Cloud operates under contractual safeguards for such transfers.

Children

BigShopR is not directed at children under 18, and under the DPDP Act we do not knowingly process a child's personal data without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

  • All traffic uses TLS.
  • Access is enforced server-side by Firestore Security Rules, not by the app. A list is readable only by accounts in its member roster; this is verified by an automated adversarial test suite on every change.
  • Invite codes are generated from a cryptographic random source, are 6 characters from a 32-symbol alphabet, and expire after whichever window the inviter chose (24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days).
  • Purchase history cannot be edited — it records what was actually bought, so nothing rewrites it after the fact. It is removed when the list it belongs to is deleted, which is what happens to a list you were the only member of when you delete your account.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
