BigShopR — Deleting your account

Last updated: 7 October 2026

You can delete your BigShopR account and its data from inside the app, at any time, without contacting us.

How

  1. Open BigShopR and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  4. You will be asked to sign in again — this is to make sure it is you.

If you have lost access to the app or cannot sign in, email contact@bagrapp.in from the address your account uses and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profileName, email, photo, and your settings. Deleted immediately.
Your purchase history in a list only you were in Goes with that list, immediately.
Lists where you are the only memberDeleted immediately, along with everything on them.
Your sign-inThe Firebase Authentication record is removed, so the account no longer exists.

What stays, and why

Lists you share with other peopleThese belong to the household, not to you alone. They stay for the remaining members. If you were the owner, ownership passes to another member so the list is not stranded.
Items other members addedNot yours to remove.
Purchase history in a list you shared It belongs to the list, not to you — it is how the remaining members see what the household buys and how often. Your name is removed from it along with the rest of your profile.
Orders already sent to a shopKept as the shop's own record of a transaction it took part in.

How long it takes

Deletion from the app is immediate. Backups are overwritten on a rolling 30-day cycle, so a copy may persist in an offline backup for up to 30 days before it is gone entirely.

If you run a shop

Deleting your account also removes your shop from BigShopR. Customers will no longer be able to find it or send it lists.
