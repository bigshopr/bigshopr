BigShopR — Deleting your account

Last updated: 16 October 2026

You can request deletion of your BigShopR account from inside the app. You must be able to sign in again, and open shop orders or unresolved shop payments can prevent completion until they are resolved. Contact us if you need help.

How

  1. Open BigShopR and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  4. You will be asked to sign in again — this is to make sure it is you.

If you have lost access to the app or cannot sign in, email contact@bigshopr.com from the address your account uses. We aim to respond within 30 days, verify your identity, and help you complete deletion or explain any records that must be retained. Never send your password, UPI PIN or bank credentials.

What is deleted

Your profileName, email, photo, and your settings. Removed when deletion completes.
Your purchase history in a list only you were in Removed with that list when deletion completes.
Lists where you are the only memberDeleted immediately, along with everything on them.
Your sign-inThe Firebase Authentication record is removed, so the account no longer exists.

What stays, and why

Lists you share with other peopleThese belong to the household, not to you alone. They stay for the remaining members. If you were the owner, ownership passes to another member so the list is not stranded.
Items other members addedNot yours to remove.
Purchase history in a list you shared It belongs to the list, not to you — it is how the remaining members see what the household buys and how often. Your profile is removed, and attribution derived from that profile shows a removed member. Account identifiers can remain in shared records; they are not all anonymized.
Orders already sent to a shopOrders and payment records remain if the shared list remains. They are removed when the list is deleted, including a sole-member list removed during account deletion. A shop may separately keep records it has already received under its own obligations.

How long it takes

The app performs deletion online after you confirm and sign in again. Wait for confirmation that it has completed. Connection failures, open orders or unresolved payment records can interrupt the process; your account may remain active and you may need to retry or contact support.

Deletion removes the records described above from the app’s active data stores. It does not automatically erase shared records that remain, information already received by an independent shop or payment provider, or previously collected crash and analytics data. Those providers apply their own retention and deletion processes. Contact us for help with retained data or questions about backup retention.

If you run a shop

Deleting your account also removes your shop from BigShopR. Customers will no longer be able to find it or send it lists. First finish or cancel open orders and confirm or resolve all outstanding payment records. Check the actual payment with the customer and your bank or UPI provider before recording its status. If a record cannot be resolved, contact us for help. Do not pay again merely to complete account deletion.
