What BigShopR is

BigShopR keeps a shopping list that several people can see and edit at once, and lets a household send that list to a nearby shop that has registered with us. It is a way to share a list and pass it to a shop. It is not a payment service, a delivery service, or a party to whatever you agree with a shop.

Lists and the people on them

A list is shared. Items you add, tick off, or remove are visible to every member, along with your name.

The owner of a list can remove members, change what editors are allowed to do, and delete the list for everyone.
