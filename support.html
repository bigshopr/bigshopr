BigShopR — Support

BigShopR is a shared shopping list that a household can send to a nearby shop.

Need help? Email contact@bigshopr.com and we will reply within two working days. Please include your phone model, your iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I invite someone to my list?
Open the list, tap the share icon, and send the code or link. They open it in BigShopR and join.

How do I send my list to a shop?
Open the Shop tab, choose Send to shops, and pick a saved shop. To add a shop, scan the code the shop shows in its own BigShopR app, or type the code in by hand. The shop replies with what it has, you accept, and you collect with a four-digit code.

Why can't I send to a shop?
Only shops that BigShopR has reviewed can receive lists. If a shop you know isn't listed, ask the shop to register in BigShopR.

Does BigShopR take payment?
You pay the shop directly with cash or UPI. UPI is available for INR orders after handover, when the shop enables it and finishes pricing the bill. Choose UPI on your order to see the shop’s saved payee details. On Android you pick from your installed UPI apps. On iPhone BigShopR opens Google Pay; to pay with PhonePe, Paytm or another app, show the QR code on another device or copy the UPI ID. BigShopR never handles money, card details, or your UPI PIN. The shop confirms receipt; returning from a UPI app or tapping “I’ve paid” does not verify payment.

If your UPI app shows a pending payment or a debit, check with the shop before paying again. Contact your UPI app or bank for payment disputes or refunds.

How do I delete my account?
Go to the You tab, scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account. See what is deleted and what stays.

I run a shop. How do I get on BigShopR?
Choose "I run a shop" when you first sign in, create your shop, and we review it before it can receive lists.
